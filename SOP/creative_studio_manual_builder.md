# SOP: Building an Email from Scratch

**Department:** Marketing  
**System:** Creative Studio  
**Effective Date:** April 3, 2026  
**Category:** Company Wide  

---

## Purpose

This SOP covers how to build an email **manually from scratch** in Creative Studio. This method gives you full creative control — you pick the blocks, arrange the layout, write the copy, and style everything exactly the way you want. It's perfect for when you have a clear vision or need a custom design.

---

## When to Use This Process

- You have a specific layout in mind that templates or AI might not match
- You want complete control over every element
- You're building something unique (like an event flyer email or custom announcement)
- You want to learn the building blocks of email design in Creative Studio

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Start a New Email

1. Open **Creative Studio** from the Marketing Hub
2. You have two options:
   - **Start blank** — Click **"New Project"** or start with an empty canvas
   - **Start from a template** — Go to the **Templates** tab, pick a template, and modify it

> **Tip:** Starting from a template and modifying it is often faster than starting completely blank. You can delete blocks you don't need and add new ones!

---

### Step 2: Add Blocks from the Block Palette

1. Click the **Blocks** tab in the left sidebar
2. You'll see a list of available block types:
   - **Header** — Logo and top navigation
   - **Hero** — Big image/text banner
   - **Text** — Simple text paragraph
   - **Image** — Full-width or inline image
   - **Button / CTA** — Clickable call-to-action button
   - **Divider** — Horizontal line separator
   - **Spacer** — Empty space between sections
   - **Columns** — Side-by-side content
   - **Footer** — Contact info and social links
   - And more!
3. **Click** a block to add it to the bottom of your email, or **drag** it to a specific position on the canvas

> **Note:** You can add as many blocks as you need. Build your email like stacking building blocks!

---

### Step 3: Edit Block Properties

1. **Click on any block** in the canvas to select it
2. The **Properties Panel** will appear on the right side of the screen
3. Depending on the block type, you can edit:
   - **Text content** — Type or paste your copy
   - **Font** — Change the font family, size, weight, and color
   - **Background color** — Set a background for the block
   - **Padding / Spacing** — Control the space around the content
   - **Images** — Upload or paste an image URL
   - **Links** — Add hyperlinks to text or buttons
   - **Button style** — Change button color, text, border radius, etc.
4. Changes appear in real-time on the canvas

> **Tip:** Click somewhere outside the block (or press Escape) to deselect it and see how it looks in context.

---

### Step 4: Rearrange Blocks

Need to move things around? Easy!

1. **Hover** over a block in the canvas — you'll see a drag handle (usually on the left or top)
2. **Click and drag** the block up or down to reorder it
3. Release the mouse button to drop it in its new position

You can also use the **Layers Panel** (see Step 5) to reorder blocks.

---

### Step 5: Use the Layers Panel

The Layers Panel gives you a bird's-eye view of your email structure:

1. Click the **Layers** tab in the left sidebar
2. You'll see a list of all blocks in your email, from top to bottom
3. From here you can:
   - **Click** a layer to select that block on the canvas
   - **Drag** layers to reorder blocks
   - **Rename** layers for easier organization
   - **Delete** blocks you no longer need
   - **Toggle visibility** if available

> **Tip:** The Layers Panel is super helpful when your email gets long. It's much easier to find and manage blocks from the list view.

---

### Step 6: Change Fonts and Global Styles

To set consistent styling across your entire email:

1. Look for the **Global Styles** or **Design** settings (usually in the top toolbar or a settings panel)
2. Here you can set:
   - **Default font family** — Applied to all text blocks
   - **Default font size** — Base size for body text
   - **Email background color** — The color behind your email
   - **Content width** — Usually 600px (email standard)
   - **Link colors** — Default color for hyperlinks
3. These global settings apply everywhere unless overridden by individual block settings

> **Tip:** Set your global styles first before editing individual blocks. This saves you from having to change the font on every single block!

---

## Expected Results

After completing these steps, you should have:
- A custom-built email with exactly the layout and content you want
- Properly styled blocks with consistent fonts and colors
- An email structure that's organized and easy to edit later

---

## Best Practices

- **Plan your layout first** — Sketch out the sections you need before you start building. A typical email might be: Header → Hero → Content → CTA → Footer.
- **Keep it simple** — The best emails have 3–5 sections. Don't overload your readers with too much.
- **Use consistent spacing** — Add Spacer or Divider blocks between sections for a clean look.
- **Stick to 600px width** — This is the email standard that works across all email clients.
- **Save frequently** — Don't lose your masterpiece! Save early and often.
- **Apply your Brand Kit** — After building, use the Brand tab to quickly apply your property's colors and fonts for a polished look.
