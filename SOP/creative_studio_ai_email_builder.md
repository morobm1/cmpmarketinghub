# SOP: Building an Email with the AI Guide

**Department:** Marketing  
**System:** Creative Studio  
**Effective Date:** April 3, 2026  
**Category:** Company Wide  

---

## Purpose

This SOP walks you through how to use the **AI Generate** feature in Creative Studio to quickly build a professional email. The AI guide takes your input — like the purpose of the email, who it's for, and the tone you want — and creates a full email draft for you in seconds. It's a great starting point, especially when you're short on time or not sure where to begin!

---

## When to Use This Process

- You need to create an email quickly and want a solid starting point
- You're not sure how to structure the email content
- You want to experiment with different tones or messaging approaches
- You'd like the AI to suggest layout and copy for you

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Open Creative Studio

1. Log in to the **CMP Marketing Hub**
2. Click **Creative Studio** from the left navigation menu (or visit the Creative Studio page directly)
3. You'll land on the main editor screen

> **Tip:** If you already have a project open, you can start a new one from the **Projects** tab or the top toolbar.

---

### Step 2: Click the AI Generate Button

1. In the **left sidebar**, look for the **AI Generate** tab (it has a sparkle/magic icon ✨)
2. Click on it to open the AI generation panel

> **Note:** The AI Generate panel will slide open on the left side of the screen.

---

### Step 3: Fill In the AI Prompt

The AI needs a little information from you to create the best email possible. Fill in these fields:

1. **Purpose / Goal** — What is this email about? (e.g., "Announce our spring lease special" or "Invite residents to a pool party")
2. **Target Audience** — Who is this email for? (e.g., "Current residents," "Prospective students," "Parents of applicants")
3. **Tone** — How should the email feel? (e.g., "Friendly and exciting," "Professional," "Urgent")
4. Optionally, add any **extra details** or specific things you want included (like a date, discount amount, or property name)

> **Tip:** The more specific you are, the better the AI result will be. Instead of "lease special," try "20% off first month's rent for fall 2026 move-ins."

---

### Step 4: Generate the Email

1. Click the **Generate** button
2. Wait a few seconds — the AI is building your email!
3. You'll see a loading indicator while it works

> **Note:** Generation usually takes 5–15 seconds depending on complexity.

---

### Step 5: Review the AI-Generated Result

Once the AI finishes, your email will appear in the **canvas** (the main editing area). Take a moment to review:

1. **Read through the copy** — Does it match your intent? Is the tone right?
2. **Check the layout** — Are the sections in a logical order?
3. **Look at the CTA (Call to Action)** — Is it clear and compelling?
4. **Check for any placeholder text** — The AI might use brackets like [Property Name] that you need to fill in

> **Tip:** Don't worry if it's not perfect — you can edit everything! The AI gives you a head start, not the final product.

---

### Step 6: Make Edits After AI Generates

Now it's time to make it yours:

1. **Click on any text block** in the canvas to select it
2. The **Properties Panel** will appear on the right side
3. Edit the text, change colors, adjust font sizes, or update links
4. You can also:
   - **Delete blocks** you don't need (select and press Delete, or use the trash icon)
   - **Rearrange blocks** by dragging them up or down
   - **Add new blocks** from the Block Palette in the left sidebar

---

### Step 7: Apply Your Brand Kit

To make the email match your property's branding:

1. Click the **Brand** tab in the left sidebar
2. Select your property's **Brand Kit** from the dropdown
3. Click **"Rebrand My Draft"**
4. The AI will update colors, fonts, logos, and other brand elements automatically

> **Tip:** If you haven't set up a Brand Kit yet, check out the **"Setting Up a Brand Kit"** SOP for instructions.

---

## Expected Results

After completing these steps, you should have a fully designed email that:
- Matches your intended purpose and audience
- Uses your property's brand colors, fonts, and logo
- Is ready to export or continue editing

---

## Best Practices

- **Start with AI, finish with your voice** — The AI is a starting point. Always personalize the content to match your property's unique vibe.
- **Try different tones** — If you don't love the first result, change the tone and regenerate. "Fun and casual" gives very different results than "professional and informative."
- **Save your work often** — Use the Save button in the top toolbar so you don't lose progress.
- **Apply brand kit last** — Make your content edits first, then apply the brand kit so everything looks polished.
