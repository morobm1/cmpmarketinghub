# SOP: Quick Start — Make Your First Email in 5 Minutes

**Department:** Marketing  
**System:** Creative Studio  
**Effective Date:** April 3, 2026  
**Category:** Company Wide  

---

## Purpose

This is your **speed-run guide** to creating your first email in Creative Studio. No fuss, no deep dives — just the fastest path from zero to a finished, branded email. Ready? Let's go!

---

## When to Use This Process

- You're brand new to Creative Studio and want to jump in fast
- You need a quick email and don't have time for a deep tutorial
- You want to see what Creative Studio can do in under 5 minutes

---

## Let's Go! (5 Steps, 5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Log In and Open Creative Studio (30 seconds)

1. Go to the **CMP Marketing Hub** and log in
2. Click **Creative Studio** in the left navigation menu
3. You're in! Welcome to the editor.

---

### Step 2: Pick a Template (1 minute)

1. Click the **Templates** tab in the left sidebar
2. Browse the template library — you'll see options for:
   - Lease specials
   - Event invitations
   - Move-in announcements
   - General marketing
3. Find one that's close to what you need and **click it** to load it onto the canvas
4. Don't worry about the colors or branding yet — we'll fix that next!

> **Tip:** Don't see the perfect template? Pick the closest one — it's faster to modify a template than to start from scratch.

---

### Step 3: Rebrand It (1 minute)

1. Click the **Brand** tab in the left sidebar
2. Select your **property's Brand Kit** from the dropdown
3. Click **"Rebrand My Draft"**
4. Watch the magic happen — colors, fonts, and logos update automatically!

> **Note:** If your property doesn't have a Brand Kit yet, check out the **"Setting Up a Brand Kit" SOP** to create one. For now, you can still manually update colors and logos.

---

### Step 4: Edit the Text and Add Your CTA (2 minutes)

1. **Click on any text block** in the canvas to select it
2. Update the **headline** — make it catchy! (e.g., "Don't Miss Our Spring Special!")
3. Update the **body text** — keep it short and sweet
4. Click on the **CTA button** and update:
   - The button text (e.g., "Apply Now" or "Schedule a Tour")
   - The button link (paste your URL)
5. Delete any blocks you don't need, add any you do

> **Tip:** Less is more! A short, punchy email with one clear CTA performs better than a long email with five different links.

---

### Step 5: Export and Send (30 seconds)

1. Click the **Export** button in the top toolbar
2. Choose **"Entrata Paste-In"** for Entrata emails (or "Full HTML" for other uses)
3. Click **"Copy"** to copy the HTML to your clipboard
4. Head over to **Entrata Message Center**, create a new message, switch to **Source/HTML view**, and paste!
5. Send a test email to yourself, review it, and you're ready to send!

You just created a professional, branded email in under 5 minutes. Not bad, right?

## Best Practices

- **Explore!** — Creative Studio has tons of features. Don't be afraid to click around and try things.
- **You can't break anything** — Every change can be undone, and you can always start over from a template.
- **Save your work** — Hit save after every major change. Future you will be grateful.
- **Ask for help** — If you get stuck, check the other SOPs or reach out to your marketing team.
- **Have fun with it!** — Email design should be creative and enjoyable. Make something you're proud of!
