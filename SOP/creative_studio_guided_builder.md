# SOP: Building an Email with the Guided Mode

**Department:** Marketing  
**System:** Creative Studio  
**Effective Date:** April 3, 2026  
**Category:** Company Wide  

---

## Purpose

This SOP explains how to use **Guided Mode** in Creative Studio to build an email step by step. Guided Mode walks you through each section of the email — from the header to the closing — so you never have to stare at a blank page. It's like having a helpful assistant ask you the right questions!

---

## When to Use This Process

- You want a structured, step-by-step approach to building an email
- You're newer to email creation and want guidance along the way
- You prefer answering prompts over starting from scratch
- You want to use CTA suggestion chips for quick button text ideas

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Open Guided Mode

1. Open **Creative Studio** from the Marketing Hub
2. In the left sidebar, click the **Guided Mode** tab (look for the **wand icon** 🪄)
3. The Guided Mode wizard will open, ready to walk you through each section

> **Tip:** Guided Mode builds your email one section at a time, so you can focus on each piece without feeling overwhelmed.

---

### Step 2: Purpose

1. The first step asks: **"What is the purpose of this email?"**
2. Type a brief description of what you're trying to communicate
   - Example: "Promote our end-of-summer move-in special"
   - Example: "Remind residents about the upcoming pool party"
3. Click **Next** to continue

---

### Step 3: Header

1. Choose your **header style** — this is the top banner/logo area of the email
2. You can:
   - Upload or select a logo
   - Pick a background color
   - Add a tagline or property name
3. Click **Next** when you're happy with it

---

### Step 4: Hero Section

1. The **Hero** is the big, eye-catching section at the top of your email
2. Add a **headline** — make it bold and attention-grabbing!
3. Add a **subheadline** or short description
4. Optionally add a **hero image** (a great photo of your property works well here)
5. Click **Next** to continue

> **Tip:** Keep your headline short — 5–8 words max. Think of it like a billboard!

---

### Step 5: Content Section

1. This is where you write the **main body** of your email
2. Type your message — tell your audience what they need to know
3. Keep paragraphs short (2–3 sentences each)
4. You can add multiple content blocks if needed
5. Click **Next** when you're done

> **Tip:** Write like you're talking to a friend. Friendly, clear, and to the point works best for student housing emails.

---

### Step 6: Call to Action (CTA)

1. Every great email needs a clear **CTA button** — this is what you want people to click!
2. Type your button text (e.g., "Apply Now," "Schedule a Tour," "Learn More")
3. **Use the CTA Suggestion Chips!** — Guided Mode shows you popular CTA options as clickable chips. Just tap one to use it:
   - 🏠 Schedule a Tour
   - 📝 Apply Now
   - 📞 Contact Us
   - 🎉 RSVP Today
   - 💰 View Specials
4. Add your **button link** (the URL where the button should go)
5. Click **Next**

---

### Step 7: Extras

1. This optional step lets you add extra sections like:
   - **Amenity highlights**
   - **Testimonials or quotes**
   - **Social media links**
   - **Additional images**
2. Pick what makes sense for your email — or skip this step if your email is already complete
3. Click **Next**

---

### Step 8: Ending / Footer

1. Choose your **footer content**:
   - Property address and contact info
   - Social media icons
   - Unsubscribe link (important for compliance!)
   - Legal disclaimers if needed
2. Click **Next** to move to the final step

---

### Step 9: Review and Apply

1. You'll see a **preview summary** of all the sections you've built
2. Review each section — you can go back to any step to make changes
3. When everything looks good, click **"Apply to Canvas"** (or similar button)
4. Your guided email will be generated and placed on the main canvas for further editing

> **Note:** After applying, you can still edit any block by clicking on it in the canvas. Guided Mode gives you the structure — you can fine-tune everything after.

---

## Expected Results

After completing Guided Mode, you should have:
- A fully structured email with header, hero, content, CTA, and footer
- Clean, organized layout ready for final edits
- Content that matches your stated purpose

---

## Best Practices

- **Don't overthink each step** — You can always edit after. Guided Mode is about getting a solid foundation quickly.
- **Use the CTA suggestion chips** — They're based on what works best for student housing marketing.
- **Apply your Brand Kit after** — Once the guided email is on the canvas, use the Brand tab to apply your property's colors and fonts.
- **Save your draft** — Hit save after the guided email is applied so you don't lose your work.
- **Explore the extras** — Amenity highlights and social links can make your email more engaging.
