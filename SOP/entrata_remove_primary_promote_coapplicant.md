## PART 2: FULL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

### 1. Purpose

This SOP outlines the process for removing a current Primary Applicant or Resident from a household and promoting an existing Co-Applicant to Primary status within Entrata. This procedure ensures accurate record-keeping, proper lease modification, and compliance with property policies.

### 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all leasing staff at [Property Name] when a household requires a primary applicant/resident change. This includes situations where:

- The current Primary is moving out but other occupants are remaining
- A household requests a change in primary responsibility
- A lease modification is required to reflect the new primary resident

This SOP does not cover situations where all occupants are vacating or where a completely new applicant is being added.

### 3. Definitions

**Primary (Old Primary):** The current primary applicant or resident who is being removed from the household and whose name appears first on the lease.

**Co-Applicant (New Primary):** An existing co-applicant or co-resident who will be promoted to Primary status and become the main responsible party on the lease.

**Lease Modification:** A status in Entrata that indicates changes are being made to an existing lease agreement. The account enters this status when household composition changes.

**Rescreening:** The process of running background, credit, and criminal checks on an applicant. May be required when a co-applicant is promoted to Primary, depending on property policy and original screening results.

### 4. Prerequisites and Required Information

Before beginning this process, leasing staff must collect and verify the following:

#### Authorization
- Confirm that the change has been approved by the appropriate authority (Property Manager, Regional Manager, or per property policy)
- Document the reason for the change
- Verify that all parties (old Primary and new Primary) are aware of and agree to the change

#### Required Information
- **Forwarding address for the old Primary** (required field in Entrata)
- Contact information for the new Primary (verify current)
- Move-out date for the old Primary
- Any special circumstances or notes related to the change

#### Compliance Requirements
- Review property policy on rescreening requirements
- Check if any additional documentation is needed per state/local regulations
- Verify lease modification fees (if applicable)
- Confirm notice period requirements have been met

### 5. Step-by-Step Procedure

#### Step 1: Locate the Resident Account

1. Log into Entrata
2. Use the **Search bar** at the top of the screen
3. Enter the old Primary's name or unit number
4. Select the correct resident account from the search results

**Note:** Ensure you have selected the correct household, especially in properties with similar names or multiple leases.

#### Step 2: Access the Household Tab

1. Once in the resident account, click on the **Household** tab
2. Review the current household composition to confirm:
   - The old Primary is listed as Primary
   - The co-applicant who will become the new Primary is listed in the household
3. Click the **Edit Occupants** button

**Troubleshooting:** If you do not see the "Edit Occupants" button, verify your user permissions with your supervisor or IT administrator.

#### Step 3: Place the Old Primary on Notice

1. In the Edit Occupants screen, locate the current Primary
2. Click on the **Notice** or **Place on Notice** option next to the old Primary's name
3. Enter the required information:
   - Notice date (typically today's date)
   - Move-out date
   - Reason for move-out (select from dropdown or enter custom reason)

**Important:** Do not skip this step. Placing the resident on notice is required before the system will allow you to change the Primary designation.

#### Step 4: Enter Forwarding Address

1. A forwarding address field will appear after placing the resident on notice
2. Enter the complete forwarding address for the old Primary:
   - Street address
   - City
   - State
   - ZIP code
3. Verify the address is accurate and complete

**Critical:** The forwarding address is a required field. The system will not allow you to proceed without this information. If the old Primary has not provided a forwarding address, you must obtain it before continuing.

#### Step 5: Assign the New Primary

1. In the same Edit Occupants screen, locate the co-applicant who will become the new Primary
2. Look for the **Primary** designation option (typically a dropdown or radio button)
3. Select or click to assign this co-applicant as the **new Primary**
4. The system will automatically update the designation

**Note:** The list of available occupants will only show current household members. You cannot assign someone who is not already part of the household.

#### Step 6: Submit the Changes

1. Review all changes on the screen:
   - Old Primary is on notice with forwarding address
   - New Primary is designated correctly
2. Click **Submit** or **Save** at the bottom of the screen
3. Wait for the system to process the changes (may take a few seconds)

**Confirmation:** You should see a success message or be returned to the Household tab with updated information.

#### Step 7: Verify Lease Modification Status

1. After submitting, the account should automatically change to **Lease Modification** status
2. Navigate to the **Application** or **Lease** tab to verify this status
3. The status indicator should show "Lease Modification" or similar

**What This Means:** Lease Modification status indicates that the system recognizes changes have been made to the household composition and a new or modified lease agreement is required.

**Troubleshooting:** If the status does not change to Lease Modification, try refreshing the page. If the issue persists, contact your supervisor or Entrata support.

#### Step 8: Determine Rescreening Requirements

**Decision Point:** Does the new Primary need to be rescreened?

Consult your property's policy on rescreening. Common scenarios include:

**Rescreening Required If:**
- The co-applicant was not fully screened initially
- The original screening is older than [X months] per property policy
- The co-applicant's financial or background information has changed
- Property policy requires rescreening for all Primary promotions

**Rescreening Not Required If:**
- The co-applicant was fully screened within the acceptable timeframe
- Property policy allows promotion without rescreening
- Manager has approved waiving rescreening (document this approval)

**If Rescreening IS Required:**
1. Navigate to the **Screening** section in Entrata
2. Initiate a new screening for the new Primary
3. Process payment for screening fees (if applicable)
4. Wait for screening results to return
5. Review results per property screening criteria
6. Approve or deny based on results
7. Document decision in resident notes

**If Rescreening IS NOT Required:**
1. Proceed directly to Step 9

#### Step 9: Approve the Application

1. Navigate to the **Application** tab
2. Locate the Lease Modification application
3. Review all information for accuracy:
   - New Primary information is correct
   - Old Primary is marked for move-out
   - Lease terms are accurate
4. Click **Approve Application**
5. Add any required notes or comments

**Note:** Some properties may require manager approval at this stage. Follow your property's approval workflow.

#### Step 10: Generate the Lease Packet

1. After approval, navigate to the **Lease** or **Documents** tab
2. Click **Generate Lease** or **Create Lease Packet**
3. Select the appropriate lease template (should auto-populate based on property settings)
4. Review the lease document to ensure:
   - New Primary's name appears as the primary leaseholder
   - Old Primary is not included on the new lease
   - All terms and dates are correct
5. Click **Generate** or **Create**
6. Send the lease packet to the new Primary for signature via Entrata's e-signature system

**Delivery Method:**
- Email notification will be sent to the new Primary
- Resident can also access documents through the resident portal
- Follow up with the resident to ensure they receive and complete the signature

#### Step 11: Wait for Lease Execution

1. Monitor the lease status in the **Documents** or **Lease** tab
2. Wait for the new Primary to sign the lease electronically
3. Once signed by the resident, the lease will route for property signatures (if required)
4. After all signatures are collected, mark the lease as **Approved** or **Executed** in Entrata

**Timeline:** Set a reminder to follow up if the lease is not signed within [X business days] per property policy.

#### Step 12: Process Move-Out for Old Primary

**Important:** This step should only be completed AFTER the new lease is fully signed and approved.

1. Return to the **Household** tab
2. Locate the old Primary (should still show as "On Notice")
3. Click **Move Out** next to the old Primary's name
4. Enter the move-out details:
   - Move-out date (should match the date entered earlier)
   - Confirm forwarding address
   - Add any final notes
5. Click **Submit** or **Complete Move-Out**

**System Action:** The old Primary will now be removed from the active household and moved to the "Past Occupants" section.

#### Step 13: Final Verification

1. Navigate back to the **Household** tab
2. Verify the following:
   - **New Primary** is listed as the Primary resident
   - **Old Primary** appears in the "Past Occupants" or "Previous Residents" section
   - No other unexpected changes have occurred
3. Check the **Lease** tab to confirm:
   - The new lease is marked as Active
   - The lease shows the correct Primary name
4. Review the **Account** tab to ensure:
   - Billing is assigned to the new Primary
   - Contact information is current

**Completion:** The process is now complete. The new Primary is the sole active resident, and the old Primary has been properly moved out in the system.

### 7. Troubleshooting Common Issues

#### Issue: "Edit Occupants" Button Not Available

**Possible Causes:**
- User permissions do not allow household editing
- Account is in a status that prevents editing (e.g., eviction, collections)

**Resolution:**
1. Contact your supervisor to verify your user role and permissions
2. If permissions are correct, check the account status
3. If account is in a restricted status, consult with management before proceeding
4. Contact Entrata support if permissions appear correct but button is still unavailable

#### Issue: Lease Modification Status Not Appearing

**Possible Causes:**
- Changes were not properly saved
- System delay in updating status
- Account configuration issue

**Resolution:**
1. Refresh the page and check again
2. Log out and log back into Entrata
3. Verify that the changes were saved by checking the Household tab
4. If changes were saved but status hasn't updated, wait 5-10 minutes and check again
5. Contact Entrata support if status does not update within 30 minutes

#### Issue: Rescreening Required Unexpectedly

**Possible Causes:**
- Property settings require rescreening for all Primary changes
- Original screening has expired per system settings
- Co-applicant was not fully screened initially

**Resolution:**
1. Review property policy on rescreening requirements
2. Check the date of the original screening in the system
3. Consult with your Property Manager on whether rescreening can be waived
4. If rescreening cannot be waived, proceed with Step 8 (rescreening process)
5. Document the reason for rescreening in the resident notes

#### Issue: Lease Packet Not Generating

**Possible Causes:**
- Application not fully approved
- Missing required information in the system
- Lease template configuration issue
- System error

**Resolution:**
1. Verify the application status is "Approved"
2. Check that all required fields are completed (unit number, lease dates, rent amount, etc.)
3. Try generating the lease again
4. Try a different lease template if multiple are available
5. Clear your browser cache and try again
6. Contact Entrata support if the issue persists

#### Issue: Signature Not Showing or E-Signature Not Sending

**Possible Causes:**
- Incorrect email address for new Primary
- Email caught in spam filter
- E-signature system not enabled
- Document not properly generated

**Resolution:**
1. Verify the new Primary's email address is correct in Entrata
2. Ask the resident to check their spam/junk folder
3. Resend the e-signature request from the Documents tab
4. Verify that e-signature is enabled for your property
5. If e-signature is not available, print the lease for wet signature
6. Contact Entrata support if technical issues persist

#### Issue: Cannot Complete Move-Out for Old Primary

**Possible Causes:**
- Outstanding balance on the account
- Lease not yet approved
- System requires additional information

**Resolution:**
1. Verify the new lease is fully executed and approved
2. Check for any outstanding balances or charges
3. Ensure forwarding address is entered
4. Consult with your Property Manager if there are unresolved financial issues
5. Document any special circumstances that prevent immediate move-out

### 8. Documentation Standards

All changes to household composition must be properly documented in Entrata. Follow these standards:

#### Where to Document

**Primary Location:** Resident Notes section in Entrata
- Access via the **Notes** or **Activity** tab in the resident account
- Use note type: "Household Change" or "Lease Modification" (if available)

**Secondary Location:** Physical resident file (if maintained)
- Print confirmation of changes
- File with lease documents

#### What to Include in Notes

Every household change note should include:

1. **Date and Time:** When the change was processed
2. **Staff Member:** Your name and title
3. **Type of Change:** "Primary Applicant Change - Removed [Old Primary Name], Promoted [New Primary Name]"
4. **Authorization:** Who approved the change (manager name, date of approval)
5. **Reason:** Brief explanation (e.g., "Old Primary relocating for work, co-applicant remaining in unit")
6. **Forwarding Address:** Document that forwarding address was collected
7. **Rescreening:** Note whether rescreening was required and results (if applicable)
8. **Move-Out Date:** Effective date for old Primary
9. **Lease Details:** New lease number, effective date, any changes to terms
10. **Special Circumstances:** Any unusual situations or exceptions made

#### Sample Note Template

```
Date: [MM/DD/YYYY]
Staff: [Your Name], Leasing Consultant
Action: Primary Applicant Change

Removed Primary: [Old Primary Full Name]
New Primary: [New Primary Full Name]
Unit: [Unit Number]

Authorization: Approved by [Manager Name] on [Date]
Reason: [Brief explanation]
Move-Out Date for Old Primary: [MM/DD/YYYY]
Forwarding Address Collected: Yes - [Address]

Rescreening: [Required/Not Required]
[If required: Results - Approved/Denied on MM/DD/YYYY]

New Lease Generated: [Lease Number]
Lease Effective Date: [MM/DD/YYYY]
Lease Signed: [MM/DD/YYYY]
Old Primary Moved Out in System: [MM/DD/YYYY]

Special Notes: [Any additional relevant information]
```

#### Document Retention

- Electronic notes: Retained permanently in Entrata
- Physical documents: Retain per property document retention policy
- Email communications: Save to resident file or forward to property management email for filing
