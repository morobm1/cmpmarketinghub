# SOP: Saving and Sharing Email Projects

**Department:** Marketing  
**System:** Creative Studio  
**Effective Date:** April 3, 2026  
**Category:** Company Wide  

---

## Purpose

This SOP covers how to **save**, **organize**, and **share** your email projects in Creative Studio. Whether you need to save a draft, create a variation of an existing email, or share your work with a colleague, this guide has you covered.

---

## When to Use This Process

- You want to save your work in progress (draft)
- You've finished an email and want to mark it as complete
- You need to create a copy or variation of an existing email
- You want to share an email with a team member for review or collaboration
- You need to find a previously saved project

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Save Your Work (Draft vs. Complete)

Creative Studio gives you two save options:

#### Save as Draft
- Click the **Save** button in the top toolbar
- Your project is saved as a **draft** — meaning it's still in progress
- Drafts appear in your Projects tab with a "Draft" label
- You can come back and edit anytime

#### Save as Complete
- When your email is finished and ready to export, look for a **"Mark Complete"** or **"Save as Complete"** option
- This signals that the email is finalized
- Completed projects may appear differently in your Projects list (e.g., with a checkmark)

> **Tip:** Save early, save often! Hit Ctrl+S (or Cmd+S on Mac) as a shortcut while working.

---

### Step 2: Rename Your Project

Good naming makes it easy to find emails later:

1. Click on the **project name** at the top of the editor (it may say "Untitled" or a default name)
2. Type a clear, descriptive name, for example:
   - "Spring 2026 Lease Special - Ivory University House"
   - "Pool Party Invite - The Flats"
   - "Move-In Checklist Email - August 2026"
3. The name saves automatically (or press Enter to confirm)

> **Tip:** Use a naming convention like: **[Topic] - [Property Name] - [Date/Season]** to keep things organized.

---

### Step 3: Save As New Copy (for Variations)

Need to create a variation of an existing email? Don't edit the original — make a copy!

1. Open the project you want to duplicate
2. Look for **"Save As"** or **"Duplicate Project"** in the top toolbar or project menu
3. Give the copy a new name (e.g., add "v2" or the new property name)
4. Make your edits on the copy — the original stays untouched

> **Tip:** This is great when you need the same email for multiple properties. Copy once, rebrand for each property!

---

### Step 4: Copy a Share Link

Want to share your email with someone else?

1. Look for the **Share** button in the top toolbar (share icon 🔗)
2. Click **"Copy Share Link"**
3. A unique URL is copied to your clipboard
4. Send this link to your colleague via email, Teams, Slack, or any messaging app
5. When they open the link, they'll be able to view (and possibly edit) the email

> **Note:** The person you share with may need to be logged into the Marketing Hub to access the project, depending on your team's permissions.

---

### Step 5: Find Saved Projects

All your saved emails live in the **Projects** tab:

1. Click the **Projects** tab in the left sidebar (or from the Creative Studio home screen)
2. You'll see a list of all your saved projects
3. Use the **search bar** to find a specific project by name
4. Filter by status (Draft, Complete) if available
5. Click on any project to open it in the editor

> **Tip:** Keep your project names descriptive so they're easy to search for later!

---

### Step 6: Open a Shared Project Link

If someone shares a Creative Studio link with you:

1. Click the link (or paste it into your browser)
2. Log in to the Marketing Hub if prompted
3. The shared email project will open in the Creative Studio editor
4. You can view, edit, or save your own copy depending on permissions

---

## Expected Results

After following these steps, you should be able to:
- Save and retrieve any email project quickly
- Create copies for variations without losing the original
- Share your work with team members seamlessly
- Keep your project library organized and searchable

---

## Best Practices

- **Name projects clearly** — You'll thank yourself later when you need to find that email from 3 months ago.
- **Save as Draft frequently** — Don't wait until you're done. Save as you go.
- **Use "Save As" for variations** — Never edit the original when you need a version for a different property or campaign.
- **Share links instead of screenshots** — Share links let your team see the actual email, not just a picture of it.
- **Clean up old projects** — Periodically review your Projects list and archive or delete outdated emails.
