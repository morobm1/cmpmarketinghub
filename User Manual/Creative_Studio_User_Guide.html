1. Welcome to Creative Studio 🎉

Creative Studio is your drag-and-drop email builder inside the CMP Marketing Hub. Think of it as Canva for property marketing emails — no design degree required! 🎓

With Creative Studio you can:

  • ✅ Build beautiful, branded emails in minutes
  • ✅ Start from ready-made templates designed for student housing
  • ✅ Drag in content blocks — images, buttons, promo banners, floor plans, and more
  • ✅ Switch between brand kits so every property stays on-brand
  • ✅ Export clean email code for Entrata Message Center
  • ✅ Share drafts with teammates for review
💡 No coding needed! Everything is visual — you click, drag, type, and style. If you can use Canva, you can use Creative Studio.

2. Getting Started 🚀

How to Get There

  1. Log into the CMP Marketing Hub with your team credentials.
  2. Click Creative Studio (or Email Generator) in the main navigation.
  3. You'll land on the Builder — your blank canvas, ready for blocks!

Know Your Toolbar

The top toolbar has five main tabs:

  • 📝 Builder — The main email canvas where you build
  • 📄 Templates — Browse and start from pre-made designs
  • 🎨 Brand Kit — Manage your property's colors, fonts, and logos
  • 🖼️ Image Library — Your uploaded images and property photos
  • 📂 Projects — Your saved email drafts and completed projects
📷 Screenshot: The top toolbar showing Builder, Templates, Brand Kit, Image Library, and Projects tabs

Preview Modes

While building, you can switch between:

  • 🖥️ Desktop view — See how the email looks on a computer
  • 📱 Mobile view — See how it looks on a phone
ℹ️ Always check mobile! Most students read email on their phone. Mobile preview helps you catch text that's too small or images that overflow.

3. Templates 📋

Don't want to start from scratch? Templates give you a head start with pre-built layouts designed specifically for student housing.

How to Use a Template

  1. Click the Templates tab in the top toolbar.
  2. Browse through the template gallery — you'll see options for leasing announcements, promo blasts, event invites, renewal reminders, and more.
  3. Click Preview on any template to see it full-size.
  4. Click Use Template to load it into your Builder canvas.
  5. All the blocks, text, and images are now yours to edit! Swap in your property's details and go.
💡 Pro move: After customizing a template for your property, save it as a new template so you can re-use it again later! (See Saving Your Work.)

Template Categories

  • 🏠 Leasing & Tours — Drive applications and tour sign-ups
  • 🎁 Promotions & Specials — Rate drops, move-in deals, referral bonuses
  • 🎉 Events — Pool parties, game nights, housing fairs
  • 🔄 Renewals — Renewal reminders and retention campaigns
  • 📣 General Marketing — Newsletters, amenity spotlights, welcome emails

4. Building Your Email 🏗️

The Builder is where the magic happens. Your email is made up of blocks stacked on top of each other — like building with LEGO® bricks!

Adding Blocks

  1. In the Builder view, look at the left sidebar. You'll see the Blocks tab listing all available content blocks grouped by category.
  2. Find the block you want (e.g., "Hero Image", "Button", "Text").
  3. Drag it from the sidebar onto your email canvas. Drop it wherever you want it to appear.
  4. The block appears on your canvas — click it to select and start editing!

Moving Blocks Around

  • 🔀 Drag & drop — Grab any block on the canvas and drag it up or down to reorder.
  • The other blocks will scoot out of the way to make room.

Editing a Block

  • Click on any block to select it. A blue outline appears around the selected block.
  • The right sidebar (Properties Panel) updates to show that block's settings.
  • Edit text, swap images, change colors, adjust padding — it's all right there!

Duplicating & Deleting

  • 📋 Duplicate — Click the copy icon on a selected block to make an exact copy right below it.
  • 🗑️ Delete — Click the trash icon to remove a block.

Undo & Redo

Made a mistake? No worries! Use the ↩️ Undo and ↪️ Redo buttons in the top toolbar. They track every change you make.

🪄 Power User Tip: Use the Layers Panel (bottom of left sidebar) to see a bird's-eye list of every block in your email. It's a great way to navigate long emails!

5. Content Blocks Guide 🧱

Here's your quick reference for every block type available in Creative Studio. Each one is designed for a specific purpose in your email.

Structure Blocks

BlockWhat It DoesWhen to Use It
Header Top section of your email — can include your logo and preheader text. Every email! This is typically the first block.
Footer Bottom section with contact info, social links, and legal/unsubscribe text. Every email! Always include a footer.
Spacer Adds blank vertical space between blocks. When you need breathing room between sections.
Divider A horizontal line to visually separate sections. Between content sections to keep things organized.
Color Bar A thin, colored strip (like a decorative accent line). Great at the top or bottom of an email for a pop of brand color.

Content Blocks

BlockWhat It DoesWhen to Use It
Text A rich text area — type paragraphs, headings, lists, bold/italic text. The workhorse of your email! Use for any body copy.
Button A clickable call-to-action button (e.g., "Apply Now", "Schedule a Tour"). Every email should have at least one! Links to your leasing page, tour scheduler, etc.
Callout Box A highlighted box for important information that pops off the page. Deadlines, important dates, key details you don't want missed.
Testimonial A styled quote block with attribution — perfect for resident reviews. Social proof! Show off what current residents love about your community.
Event Details A structured event card with date, time, location, and RSVP info. Resident events, open houses, pool parties, move-in orientations.
Numbered Steps A numbered step-by-step list with a clean layout. Move-in checklists, "How to Apply" steps, renewal process guides.

Media Blocks

BlockWhat It DoesWhen to Use It
Logo Displays your property logo image. Top of the email or within headers.
Hero Image A big, full-width banner image that grabs attention. Near the top of every email — your main visual hook!
Image Gallery A row of 2–3 images side by side. Showcasing amenities, property photos, or room views.
Social Links A row of social media icons (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, etc.) with links. In or near the footer. Connect residents to your social channels!
Branded Header ⚠️ A fancy header with a background image, color overlay, and logo on top. Looks amazing, but has an Entrata/Outlook compatibility warning — see note below.

Layout Blocks

BlockWhat It DoesWhen to Use It
Image + Text Side-by-side layout with an image on one side and text on the other. Amenity spotlights, feature highlights, staff introductions.
Two Columns A two-column layout for any side-by-side content. Comparing floor plans, listing two different perks, before/after info.

Property Marketing Blocks

BlockWhat It DoesWhen to Use It
Amenities A grid of amenity icons with labels — pool, gym, study rooms, etc. Highlighting what makes your property awesome.
Floor Plan Spotlight A featured floor plan card with image, bed/bath info, pricing, and a link. When you want to push a specific floor plan that has availability.
Promo Banner A bold, eye-catching promotional banner with big text and a button. Leasing specials, rate drops, limited-time offers.
Promo Bar (Skinny) A thin, one-line promotional strip — less dramatic than the full banner. Quick one-liners: "Rates starting at $X!" or "Limited spots remaining!"
Virtual Tour ⚠️ A virtual tour link/preview block. Linking to Matterport or other tours — but see the warning below.
⚠️ Entrata Compatibility Warnings

Two blocks have yellow warning triangles:
  • Branded Header ⚠️ — Uses a background image effect that doesn't display in Outlook or Entrata Message Center. The email won't break, but the background image won't show. Use a Hero Image block instead for guaranteed compatibility.
  • Virtual Tour ⚠️ — The tour preview won't render in most email clients. The link still works! But instead, consider using a Hero Image with a screenshot of the tour and a Button linking to the tour URL.

6. Brand Kit 🎨

Brand Kits keep every property's emails looking consistent and professional. Each brand kit stores a property's colors, fonts, logo, and images.

Switching Brand Kits

  1. In the Builder, open the Brand tab in the left sidebar (look for the 🎨 palette icon).
  2. Click the dropdown at the top of the panel to see all available brand kits.
  3. Select a different property's brand kit.
  4. The panel updates to show that brand kit's colors, fonts, and images.

Using Brand Kit Colors & Fonts

When you select a block and open its properties, you'll see color pickers and font selectors that pull from your active brand kit. This makes it easy to stay on-brand without memorizing hex codes!

Dragging Brand Kit Images

Your brand kit can store property photos, logos, and floor plan images. You can drag images directly from the Brand Panel onto your email canvas — they'll automatically drop into image blocks!

✨ "Rebrand My Draft"

This is the magic button! If you built an email for one property and need to adapt it for another:

  1. Switch to the new property's brand kit using the dropdown.
  2. Click the ✨ Rebrand My Draft button (with the wand icon).
  3. Confirm by clicking again — Creative Studio will update all the colors and fonts in your email to match the new brand kit automatically!
💡 Time saver! Build one great email, then use "Rebrand My Draft" to quickly create versions for multiple properties. Just swap out the property-specific text and images after rebranding.

7. Stored Links 🔗

Tired of hunting for the same URLs over and over? The Stored Links feature (also called the Link Bank) lets you save frequently-used URLs so they're always one click away.

How It Works

  • When editing a Button block, look for the Stored Links dropdown in the properties panel.
  • Select a saved link (e.g., your property's application page, tour scheduler, or website).
  • The URL is automatically filled in — no copy-pasting needed!
ℹ️ Common stored links for student housing:
  • Application page
  • Tour scheduling link
  • Virtual tour URL
  • Floor plans page
  • Property website homepage
  • Social media profiles

8. CTA Library 💬

Stuck on what your button should say? The CTA Library (Call-to-Action Library) is your built-in copywriting assistant with hundreds of proven button phrases organized by category!

How to Use It

  1. Click on a Button block to select it.
  2. In the properties panel, look for the CTA suggestion chips — they appear as clickable tags below the button text field.
  3. Click any suggestion to instantly use it as your button text!

CTA Categories

The library organizes CTAs by purpose so you can find the perfect phrase:

  • 🏠 General Leasing — "Apply Now", "Secure Your Spot", "Find Your Floorplan"
  • 🔥 Urgency / FOMO — "Limited Spaces Left", "Last Chance to Lease"
  • 🚶 Tour — "Schedule a Tour", "Come See It for Yourself"
  • 🌐 Virtual Tour — "Take a Virtual Tour", "Tour From Anywhere"
  • ✅ Apply / Conversion — "Start Your Application", "Make It Official"
  • 💰 Rate / Promo — "Lock In Special Pricing", "Claim This Limited-Time Offer"
  • ⏳ Waitlist — "Join the Waitlist", "Be First to Know"
  • 📞 Contact — "Contact Us", "Ask a Question", "Let's Chat"
  • 🛋️ Amenity — "Explore the Amenities", "Live With More"
  • 📍 Location — "Live Close to Campus", "Walk to Class"
  • 🔄 Renewal — "Renew Today", "Keep Your Spot", "Stay Another Year"
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent-Focused — "Give Your Student a Great Place to Live"
  • 👯 Roommate / Group — "Sign With Your Roommates", "Lease Together"
🪄 Pro Tip: The best email buttons are short (2–5 words), action-oriented, and create a sense of urgency. "Apply Now" beats "Click Here to Submit an Application" every time!

9. Images 🖼️

Great emails need great images! Creative Studio gives you multiple ways to add visuals.

Adding Images to Blocks

  1. Click on an image-capable block (Hero Image, Logo, Image Gallery, Image + Text, etc.).
  2. In the properties panel, you'll see an Image URL field.
  3. Paste in the URL of your image, or use the Image Library to browse uploaded images.

Using the Image Library

  • Click the Image Library tab in the top toolbar to browse all your uploaded property photos.
  • You can search, filter, and preview images.
  • Click an image to copy its URL, then paste into any image field.

Drag from Brand Kit

If your brand kit has property photos, logos, or floor plan images loaded:

  • Open the Brand tab in the left sidebar.
  • Scroll down to the images section.
  • Drag any image directly onto the canvas — it will drop right into the block!
💡 Image Tips:
  • Use landscape/horizontal images for hero banners (at least 600px wide).
  • Keep file sizes reasonable — under 200KB per image is ideal for email.
  • Always fill in the alt text field — it describes the image for accessibility and shows if the image doesn't load.

10. Background Removal ✨

Need a clean cutout of a person, product, or logo? Creative Studio has a built-in background remover powered by smart image processing — right in your browser!

How to Use It

  1. Select an image block that has an image loaded.
  2. In the properties panel, look for the ✨ Remove Background button.
  3. Click it! The tool will process the image (it may take a few seconds the first time as it loads the engine).
  4. The background is removed and you're left with a clean cutout! 🎉
ℹ️ Good to know:
  • Works best with clear subjects — people, objects, logos on solid backgrounds.
  • The first time you use it in a session, it takes a moment to load the processing engine. After that, it's faster!
  • The original image is not changed — a new version is created.

11. Fonts & Styling 🖌️

Make your emails match your property's personality with custom fonts, colors, and spacing.

Changing Fonts

  • Select a text-based block (Text, Button, Callout Box, etc.).
  • In the properties panel, find the Font Family dropdown.
  • Choose from available fonts. If a brand kit is active, the brand's preferred fonts appear at the top!

Changing Colors

  • Click any color swatch in the properties panel (text color, background color, button color, etc.).
  • A color picker appears. You can:
    • Pick from the visual color wheel
    • Type in a color code (like #446472)
    • Choose from your brand kit colors if one is active

Adjusting Padding & Spacing

  • Each block has padding controls — these adjust the space inside the block (between the content and the block's edges).
  • Use the Spacer block for adding space between blocks.

Global Styles

Some styles apply to the entire email, not just individual blocks:

  • Background color — The color behind the whole email
  • Content width — How wide the email body is
  • Default font — The base font used across the email
💡 Keep it simple! Stick to 1–2 fonts max per email. Use your brand's primary font for headings and a clean readable font for body text.

12. Saving Your Work 💾

Creative Studio gives you multiple ways to save depending on where you are in the process.

Save Options

OptionWhat It DoesWhen to Use It
💾 Save Draft Saves your email as a work-in-progress. You can come back and edit it anytime. You're still working on the email and it's not ready to send yet.
✅ Save Complete Marks the email as finished. It's still editable, but flagged as "complete" in your projects list. The email is approved and ready to export to Entrata.
📋 Save As New Copy Creates a brand new copy of the current email — the original stays untouched. You want to make a variation (e.g., same email for a different property or a different promo).

How to Save

  1. Click the 💾 Save button in the top-right of the toolbar.
  2. A small menu appears with your save options.
  3. Choose the option that fits your situation.
  4. Look for the ✅ confirmation flash — your work is saved!
⚠️ Don't forget to save! You'll see a yellow "Unsaved" badge next to your project name when you have changes that haven't been saved yet.

Naming Your Project

Click on the project name in the top toolbar to rename it anytime. Use clear, descriptive names like: "The Vue – April Move-In Special" or "Aspire – Fall 2026 Leasing Kickoff".

13. Sharing 🔗

Need a teammate or manager to review your email before it goes out? Use the share feature!

How to Share

  1. Click the 🔗 Share button in the top toolbar.
  2. A share link is generated and copied to your clipboard.
  3. Paste the link in Slack, Teams, email, or wherever your team communicates.
  4. Anyone with the link can view the email — they'll see a read-only preview.
ℹ️ Good to know: The share link is for viewing only — other people can see the email but can't edit your draft. Your work is safe!

14. Exporting for Entrata 📤

When your email is ready to send, you'll export it and paste it into Entrata Message Center.

How to Export

  1. Click the 📤 Export button in the top toolbar.
  2. The Export window opens with two options:
    • Full HTML Document — A complete standalone email file. Good for downloading and testing.
    • Entrata Paste-In — Optimized code ready to paste directly into the Entrata Message Center source editor. This is the one you'll use most!
  3. Select Entrata Paste-In.
  4. Click 📋 Copy to Clipboard.
  5. Open Entrata Message Center → Create new message → Click the Source/Code button in the editor → Paste → Done!

Export Warnings

Creative Studio will show you warnings if it detects issues before you export:

  • 🖼️ Hero Image block with no image loaded
  • 🖼️ Logo block with no image loaded
  • 🔗 Button with no URL (or placeholder URL)
  • 📝 Missing alt text on images
💡 Always fix warnings before exporting! Missing images show as blank space, broken links frustrate prospects, and missing alt text hurts accessibility.

Downloading the HTML File

You can also click ⬇️ Download to save the email as an HTML file on your computer. This is handy for archiving or sending to someone who needs the raw file.

15. Tips & Tricks 🧠

Level up your student housing email game with these best practices!

📐 Email Structure Best Practices

  • Keep it scannable. Use short paragraphs, bullet points, and clear headings. Students skim — make the key info pop!
  • One main CTA per email. What's the #1 thing you want the reader to do? Make that button big and obvious.
  • Put the important stuff first. Your hero image and main message should be visible without scrolling.
  • Mobile-first mindset. Over 70% of student housing email opens happen on phones. Always check mobile preview!

✍️ Writing Tips

  • Subject lines matter most. Keep them under 50 characters, create curiosity or urgency. Emojis in subject lines can boost open rates! 🎯
  • Write like you talk. Friendly and conversational beats formal and stiff. You're talking to 18–22 year olds!
  • Use numbers. "Starting at $799/bed" is more compelling than "Affordable rates available."
  • Create urgency. "Only 3 spots left!" and "Offer ends Friday" drive action.

🎨 Design Tips

  • Less is more. Don't use every block type in one email — pick 4–6 blocks that serve your message.
  • Consistent branding. Use your brand kit colors and fonts. When in doubt, use the Rebrand feature!
  • High-quality images. Blurry or dark photos hurt credibility. Use your best property shots.
  • White space is your friend. Use Spacer blocks to give content room to breathe.

16. Troubleshooting 🔧

Running into an issue? Here are solutions to the most common problems.

ProblemSolution
I can't drag blocks onto the canvas Make sure you're in the Builder view (first tab). Drag from the left sidebar block list — click and hold, then drag to the canvas area.
My image isn't showing up Check that the image URL is correct and publicly accessible. Private or expired URLs won't load. Try opening the URL directly in a new browser tab to test.
The email looks different in Entrata than in the builder Some styling differences are normal between the builder preview and Entrata.
  • Avoid blocks with ⚠️ warnings (Branded Header, Virtual Tour) for Entrata sends.
  • Use the Entrata Paste-In export option, not Full HTML Document.
  • Always send a test email from Entrata before blasting to your full list.
The "Unsaved" badge won't go away Click the Save button and choose a save option. If you're seeing an error message, check your internet connection and try again.
Background removal is taking forever The first use in a session takes longer because it loads the processing engine. Subsequent uses should be faster. If it stalls, refresh the page and try again.
I accidentally deleted a block Click the ↩️ Undo button in the toolbar! It will bring back your deleted block.
My brand kit colors aren't showing Make sure you've selected the right brand kit from the dropdown in the Brand panel. If no brand kits appear, your property may need one set up — ask your marketing manager.
"Rebrand My Draft" changed too much Undo right away (↩️ Undo)! Rebrand replaces colors and fonts globally. You can always undo it and do a more targeted manual update instead.
The export warnings mention missing alt text Click on each image block and fill in the Alt Text field with a short description of the image (e.g., "Aerial view of The Vue pool area"). This is important for accessibility!
I need to copy an email to another property Use Save As New Copy, then switch the brand kit and hit Rebrand My Draft. Update the property-specific text and images, and you're done!
